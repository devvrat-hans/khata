# Code of conduct

Khata is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project) is expected to be kind and respectful.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and be specific.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call, and that "not now" is not "never".

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **support@aback.ai**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban people from the project for behaviour they consider harmful.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
