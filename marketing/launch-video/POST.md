## Post

Introducing Khata: split shared costs without the awkward math.

Trips, flats, friends. Split equally, exactly, by percent or by shares. Keep something private between two people, inside a group. Settle in fewer payments, with UPI.

Free and open source.
khata.aback.group

## Thread

1. The part no other app does: a private hisab inside a group.

Sharing a cab with one person on a six person trip? Record it between just the two of you. It stays out of the group's list, balances, feed and export. The server checks it on every request.

2. Khata combines debts so a group settles with as few payments as it can. Same money, fewer transfers.

Add your UPI ID and people who owe you get a payment link with the amount filled in.

3. MIT licensed, built on Cloudflare Workers and D1. Read every line, or run your own.

github.com/devvrat-hans/khata
